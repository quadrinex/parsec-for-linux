# Final report (2026-10-30)

## What works, proven
- The official Windows Parsec app runs under Wine (`wine-plh`: wine-staging 11.20 + 4 patches, dedicated prefix), account logged in, host visible.
- A client sees and controls the real KDE desktop, on the local network and from the outside (user validation: image, mouse on the 3 screens, keyboard, sound, screen switching; rechecked after phase 2: "it works").
- Real hardware encoding: Parsec → our `amfrt64.dll` → `plh-bridge` → VA-API RX 6600.
- `bin/parsec-linux-host` start|stop|restart|status|enable|disable|config; optional user systemd service (disabled).
- `make test-full`: PASS=14 FAIL=0 SKIP=0 (2026-10-31, on the English build); `make test`: PASS=8 SKIP=6 (intrusive); bridge 7/7.
- Real `uninstall.sh --keep-prefix`: packages, udev, modules-load identical to the reference; `install.sh --system-only` restores everything.

## Measurements
- Last session (17:32, phase 2 code): 58.8–60 FPS, L 13.0–15.3 ms according to the Parsec log (L = latency reported by Parsec, not the encoding latency alone).
- Encoding alone: H.264 1080p p50 3.16 ms, p95 4.12 ms (native bench); through the AMF API under Wine, p50 4.5 ms at 720p.
- Capture: 59.5–59.9 fps on 1 screen; 29 fps on 3 simultaneous screens.

## Limits
- 3 screens captured at the same time: about 29 fps; DMA-BUF zero-copy not done.
- Gamepads: virtual Xbox 360 pad proven with a ViGEmClient-like probe (`src/input/tests/gamepad-e2e.sh`, 15/15); not proven with a real Parsec client; no rumble. Clipboard: text, never tried with a real client.
- `SetCursorPos` not relayed (D-015).
- Causes not isolated: green screen (empty 1st frame or switch to AMF), -6023 (patch 0004 probable), cursor jump to 0,31 (not reproduced).
- Encode module tests: the x264 strict-decode case failed 2 of about 8 full runs (19 frames decoded instead of 20). Not reproduced in about 60 isolated encodes; cause unknown. This is the software fallback, not the hardware path.
- Autostart at boot (2026-10-31): Wine kept an overlapping layout (DISPLAY2 and DISPLAY3 both at 1920,0), giving the wrong screen for the mouse and a duplicate in Parsec's list. A host restart fixes it (checked with `hello.exe`). `start` now waits for a stable layout; not yet verified on a real boot.
- Screen changes while running: the input hook resends the layout on WM_DISPLAYCHANGE (5 synthetic messages gave one resend, dry run). Not tested with a real screen change: whether Wine delivers that message or updates its own layout is unproven.
- Cursor: Parsec fetches our DXGI shape (session counters, 18 and 25 calls), but no cursor appeared on the phone client. The phone may hide it; not tried from a PC client. Embedded-in-video mode was tried and dropped at the user's request.
- Tray, app entry, settings window: `t07-launcher-tray`; not yet checked by eye or across a login. Wine's own blank tray item remains next to ours.
- `make test-full` not rerun since the gamepad, cursor and watcher changes; last non-visible `make test`: PASS=10 FAIL=0 SKIP=7.
- Patch 0003 has not been verified on a real WebSocket drop.
- t10, t11, t12 require that no keep-above window hides the test pattern.
- The uinput input service injects into the real session while a client is connected.
